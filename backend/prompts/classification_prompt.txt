You are given $n_images photo(s) of a house listing. The images are labelled in order: $image_labels.

For each image, determine what type of room or area is shown. Choose from these categories: $room_types, other.

IMPORTANT — distinguishing kitchens from bathrooms:
- "kitchen": Must contain cooking/food-preparation features such as a stovetop, hob, oven, microwave, fridge, dishwasher, kitchen sink with draining area, or countertops with kitchenware. Modern minimalist kitchens still count — look for sleek integrated countertops, handleless cabinets, an induction hob, or a built-in coffee machine, even if the design is very clean and sparse.
- "bathroom": Must contain bathing/hygiene fixtures such as a shower, bathtub, toilet, bidet, or a washbasin/vanity with a mirror above it. The presence of tiles alone does NOT make a room a bathroom. If you see a sink with a tap but NO mirror, soap dispenser, or toiletries, and there ARE kitchen appliances or food-prep surfaces nearby, it is a kitchen.
- When a room has BOTH a sink and tiled surfaces, look for the dominant function: cooking appliances → kitchen; bathing/hygiene fixtures → bathroom.

IMPORTANT — distinguishing private outdoor spaces from shared/exterior areas:
- "balcony": Use ONLY for a PRIVATE balcony that belongs to the property. It will have direct access from the apartment (a door or window connection), railings/barriers, and often personal furniture or plants. The photo should prominently feature the balcony surface and boundaries.
- "garden": Use ONLY for a PRIVATE garden that belongs to the property. It will be an enclosed or fenced area directly attached to the house/apartment, clearly delineated from communal grounds.
- "exterior": Use for ALL of the following — they are NOT balconies or gardens:
  • Aerial or drone shots of the building or neighbourhood
  • Views of canals, waterways, streets, or public spaces
  • Communal or shared outdoor areas (e.g. a shared courtyard or communal garden)
  • Photos of nearby amenities (cafés, parks, town squares, shops)
  • Photos taken FROM a balcony that mainly show the surrounding view rather than the balcony itself
  • Any outdoor image where the space is clearly NOT private to this property

When in doubt, prefer "exterior" over "balcony" or "garden".

If multiple images clearly show the same room from different angles, group them together by giving them the same "group_id" (an integer starting from 1). Images of different rooms should have different group IDs.

Respond with ONLY a JSON array (no extra text) where each element has exactly these keys:
  - "label": the image label (e.g. "image_1")
  - "room_type": one of the categories above
  - "confidence": a number between 0.0 and 1.0
  - "description": a brief one-sentence description of what you see in the image
  - "group_id": an integer grouping images of the same room together
